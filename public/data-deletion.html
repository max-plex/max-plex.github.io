Account & Data Deletion Request

Manage your data privacy rights • Learn how your data is permanently removed

1. Overview of Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Store Policies and user privacy laws (including GDPR and CCPA), MaxPlex provides users with full autonomy over their personal information. You have the right to permanently erase your user account and all associated synchronized data from our production databases at any time.

2. What Data Is Permanently Erased

Data Category Specific Data Items Deletion Retention
User Profile Full Name, Email address, Profile avatar, Google Unique ID (sub) Permanently deleted immediately
Authentication Sessions Active JWT access tokens, refresh token hashes, device IDs Revoked & erased immediately
Watch Progress & History Playback timestamps (seconds/percentage), completed status Purged completely
Preferences & Favorites Saved watchlist movies, series bookmarks, genre affinity Purged completely
Device Pairing & FCM TV pairing tokens, push notification tokens, IP logs Purged completely

3. Method A: Instant In-App Account Deletion

  1. Open the MaxPlex App on your Android Mobile, Android TV, or Windows device.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Account / Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete My Account".
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your account and all stored history will be immediately and irreversibly purged from our servers.
